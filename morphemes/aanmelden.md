From a morphological perspective, the Dutch word **`aanmelden`** can be segmented into three distinct morphemes. It is a **separable verb** (*scheidbaar werkwoord*), which is a specific grammatical category in Dutch and Germanic languages.

Here is the breakdown:

### 1. Segmentation
**`aan`** + **`meld`** + **`en`**

---

### 2. Morpheme Analysis

#### **A. `aan` (Prefix)**
*   **Type:** Derivational Prefix (Separable).
*   **Origin:** Preposition/Adverb.
*   **Meaning:** Literally "on," "at," or "to."
*   **Function:**
    *   **Directionality/Completion:** It modifies the base verb to imply directing an action *towards* a specific target or authority. While *melden* means "to report," *aanmelden* implies "to report **to** someone" or "to register."
    *   **Separability:** In Dutch syntax, this prefix separates from the verb stem in main clauses.
        *   *Infinitive:* Ik wil me **aanmelden**. (I want to register.)
        *   *Main Clause:* Ik **meld** me **aan**. (I register.)
    *   **Stress:** In separable verbs, the primary stress falls on the prefix (**AAN**melden), distinguishing it from inseparable verbs where the stress falls on the stem (e.g., *beMELDen*).

#### **B. `meld` (Root / Stem)**
*   **Type:** Lexical Root (Free Morpheme).
*   **Origin:** Old Dutch *meldian*, Proto-Germanic *\*meldōną*.
*   **Meaning:** To report, to announce, to notify, or to make known.
*   **Function:** This carries the core semantic content of the word. It is the base upon which the prefix and suffix operate.
*   **Conjugation:** This stem changes during conjugation (e.g., past tense *meldde*, past participle *gemeld*).

#### **C. `en` (Suffix)**
*   **Type:** Inflectional Suffix (Infinitive Marker).
*   **Function:**
    *   **Verbalizer:** It marks the word as a verb.
    *   **Infinitive Form:** It specifically indicates the dictionary form (the infinitive).
    *   **Productivity:** This is a highly productive suffix in Dutch used to create verbs from nouns or adjectives (e.g., *werk* [work] $\rightarrow$ *werken* [to work]).
*   **Note:** In other grammatical forms, this suffix disappears or changes (e.g., *hij meldt* [he reports], *ik meld* [I report]).

---

### 3. Linguistic Context & Usage

**1. Valency and Reflexivity:**
While not a morpheme within the word itself, *aanmelden* is frequently used as a **reflexive verb** (*zich aanmelden*). The reflexive pronoun *zich* is required to indicate that the subject is performing the action on themselves (registering themselves).
*   *Example:* "Ik **meld** me **aan**." (Literally: I report myself on.)

**2. Semantic Shift:**
The addition of the prefix `aan` creates a specific semantic shift known as **directional derivation**.
*   *Melden:* To give information (general).
*   *Aanmelden:* To give information *to a system/authority* (specific registration).

**3. Comparison with Inseparable Verbs:**
To understand the morphology of `aanmelden`, it helps to contrast it with an inseparable verb like `beMELDen` (to inform someone).
*   **`aan`-melden:** Stress on prefix. Separable. (To register).
*   **`be`-melden:** Stress on stem. Inseparable. (To inform).
*   This distinction is crucial for Dutch morphology and syntax.
