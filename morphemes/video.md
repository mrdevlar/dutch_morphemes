*   **Morpheme:** `video`
*   **Type:** **Free Root Morpheme**

**Why is it monomorphemic?**
Although it comes from Latin, it has been fully **borrowed** (loanword) into Dutch. It has undergone **phonological integration** (pronounced with Dutch phonology) and **orthographic integration** (spelled as a standard Dutch noun). Therefore, linguistically, it is a single lexical item.

### 2. Diachronic Analysis (Etymology)
While it is one morpheme in Dutch, its history reveals a compound structure from its source language, Latin. This is relevant for historical linguistics but does not reflect the mental grammar of a modern Dutch speaker.

*   **Origin:** Latin verb *vidēre* ("to see").
*   **Latin Form:** *video* (1st person singular present indicative active).
*   **Latin Breakdown:**
    1.  **`vid-`**: The verbal root meaning "to see."
    2.  **`-eo`**: The inflectional ending indicating "I" (1st person singular) + present tense.
*   **Semantic Shift:** In Latin, it meant "I see." In Dutch (and English), the meaning shifted from the *action* of seeing to the *medium* or *recording* that allows one to see (visual recording).
