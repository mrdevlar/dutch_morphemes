### 2. Diachronic Analysis (Etymology & History)

**The Connection to "Zitten" (To Sit):**
The verb *zitten* (to sit) and the word *zat* share the same etymological root.
*   **Infinitive:** *zitten* (Root: *zit-*)
*   **Past Tense (Singular):** *zat* (Root: *zat-*)

In Germanic strong verbs, the past tense is often formed by **ablaut** (vowel gradation) rather than adding a suffix. The vowel shifts from *i* (in *zit*) to *a* (in *zat*). Historically, `zat` was the past tense form of the verb. Over time, it grammaticalized into an adjective.
