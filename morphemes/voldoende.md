From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`voldoende`** is a complex word formed through compounding and derivation. It is historically the present participle of the verb *voldoen*, but it has lexicalized into an independent adjective and noun.

Here is the morphological breakdown and explanation.

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented into three distinct morphemes:

> **`vol`** + **`doen`** + **`-ende`**

### 2. Morpheme Analysis

#### A. The Prefix/First Element: `vol`
*   **Category:** Adjective / Adverb
*   **Meaning:** "Full"
*   **Function:** In this compound, it acts as a modifier indicating completeness or intensity.
*   **Etymology:** Cognate with the English word *full* and the German *voll*.
*   **Semantic Contribution:** It implies that an action is carried out to completion.

#### B. The Root/Second Element: `doen`
*   **Category:** Verb
*   **Meaning:** "To do"
*   **Function:** This is the lexical root of the action.
*   **Etymology:** Cognate with the English word *do* and the German *tun*.
*   **Semantic Contribution:** It provides the core action.

#### C. The Suffix: `-ende`
*   **Category:** Derivational Suffix (Present Participle Marker)
*   **Meaning:** "-ing" (in the sense of an active state)
*   **Function:** This suffix turns the verb into a **present participle** (*tegenwoordig deelwoord*). In Dutch, present participles frequently function as adjectives or nouns.
*   **Phonology:** It adds a syllable and places the stress on the preceding syllable (the root).

---

### 3. Structural & Semantic Development

To understand the word fully, we must look at how these morphemes combine in layers:

#### Layer 1: The Compound Verb (`voldoen`)
Before the suffix is added, `vol` and `doen` combine to form the verb **`voldoen`**.
*   **Literal Meaning:** "To do fully."
*   **Actual Meaning:** "To fulfill," "to satisfy," or "to comply with."
*   **Linguistic Note:** This is a semantic shift. Just as English "fulfill" comes from "full" + "fill," Dutch `voldoen` implies doing something so completely that a requirement is met.
    *   *Example:* *Ik voldoe aan de eisen* (I fulfill the requirements).

#### Layer 2: The Participle (`voldoende`)
The suffix `-ende` is attached to the verb stem `voldoen`.
*   **Form:** `voldoen` + `-ende` → `voldoende`
*   **Grammatical Category:** Present Participle.
*   **Lexicalization:** Over time, this participle has become a standalone lexical item. It is no longer just describing an action in progress ("satisfying"); it has acquired a specific meaning of "sufficient" or "enough."

---

### 4. Grammatical Functions in Modern Dutch
Because of its morphological history, `voldoende` functions in three distinct ways in modern Dutch:

1.  **As an Adjective:** Meaning "sufficient" or "adequate."
    *   *Example:* *Er is **voldoende** tijd.* (There is **sufficient** time.)
2.  **As a Noun:** Meaning "a pass" (specifically in an academic context, a grade of 5.5 or higher).
    *   *Example:* *Hij heeft een **voldoende** gehaald.* (He got a **pass**.)
3.  **As a Verb Form (Participle):** Meaning "satisfying."
    *   *Example:* *Een **voldoende** antwoord.* (A **satisfying** answer.)
