From a morphological perspective, the Dutch word **`eeuwigheid`** (meaning "eternity") is a complex word formed through a process of derivation. It consists of three distinct morphemes.

Here is the breakdown:

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented as follows:

> **`eeuw`** + **`-ig`** + **`-heid`**

### 2. Detailed Analysis of Morphemes

#### A. The Root: `eeuw`
*   **Type:** Free morpheme (Lexeme / Noun).
*   **Meaning:** Literally "century," "age," or "era."
*   **Function:** This is the semantic core of the word. While in modern Dutch *eeuw* usually refers to a specific 100-year period, in this context, it draws upon its older, broader meaning of a vast span of time or an epoch.
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Old Dutch *ēw*, from Proto-Germanic **\*aiwaz* (time, age, eternity).
    *   *Cognates:* English *ever* (related to time), Latin *aevum* (age/eternity), German *Ewigkeit* (via the same root).

#### B. The First Suffix: `-ig`
*   **Type:** Derivational Suffix.
*   **Category Change:** Noun $\rightarrow$ Adjective.
*   **Meaning:** "Having the quality of," "full of," or "-y."
*   **Function:** It transforms the noun *eeuw* into the adjective *eeuwig* (eternal, age-old).
*   **Linguistic Note:** This is a productive suffix in Dutch. It is cognate with the English suffix *-y* (e.g., *cloud* $\rightarrow$ *cloudy*) or *-ish* (e.g., *child* $\rightarrow$ *childish*).
*   **Intermediate Word:** *eeuwig* (eternal).

#### C. The Second Suffix: `-heid`
*   **Type:** Derivational Suffix.
*   **Category Change:** Adjective $\rightarrow$ Abstract Noun.
*   **Meaning:** "-hood," "-ness," or "-ity."
*   **Function:** It nominalizes the adjective *eeuwig*, turning the quality of being "eternal" into the abstract concept of "eternity."
*   **Linguistic Note:** This is a very common nominalizer in Dutch and German.
    *   *Cognates:* English *-hood* (e.g., *child* $\rightarrow$ *childhood*), German *-heit* (e.g., *Freiheit* from *frei*).
*   **Final Word:** *eeuwigheid* (eternity).

### 3. Morphological Process Summary

The word formation follows a specific hierarchy of derivation:

1.  **Base:** `eeuw` (Noun: Century/Age)
2.  **Derivation 1:** `eeuw` + `-ig` $\rightarrow$ `eeuwig` (Adjective: Eternal)
3.  **Derivation 2:** `eeuwig` + `-heid` $\rightarrow$ `eeuwigheid` (Abstract Noun: Eternity)

### 4. Orthographic and Phonological Notes
*   **No Allomorphy:** In this specific word, the morphemes join without phonological changes (no consonant doubling, dropping, or vowel shifts). The *w* from the root remains intact before the suffix *-ig*.
*   **Stress:** In Dutch, the primary stress typically falls on the root syllable. Therefore, the stress is on the first syllable: **`eeu`**-wigheid.
