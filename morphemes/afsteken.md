From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`afsteken`** is a complex verb formed through derivation and compounding. It is classified as a **separable verb** (*scheidbaar werkwoord*).

Here is the morphological breakdown and explanation of its components.

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented into three distinct morphemes:

> **`af`** + **`stek`** + **`-en`**

| Morpheme | Type | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`af-`** | Prefix (Separable) | Modifies direction/aspect |
| **`stek-`** | Root (Stem) | Carries the core lexical meaning |
| **`-en`** | Suffix (Inflectional) | Marks the infinitive form |

---

### 2. Detailed Analysis of Morphemes

#### A. The Prefix: `af-`
*   **Type:** Separable Prefix (*scheidbaar voorvoegsel*).
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Old Dutch *af*, cognate with German *ab* and English *off*.
*   **Semantic Function:** It indicates **separation**, **downward movement**, or **completion**.
    *   In the context of *afsteken*, it often implies "setting something off" (like a fire) or "standing apart from" (contrast).
*   **Syntactic Behavior:** In Dutch main clauses, this prefix detaches from the verb and moves to the end of the sentence.
    *   *Example:* "Ik **steek** de kaars **af**." (I light the candle.)
    *   *Note:* In subordinate clauses or infinitive constructions, it remains attached: "...dat ik de kaars **afsteek**."

#### B. The Root: `stek-`
*   **Type:** Verbal Stem (*stam*).
*   **Base Verb:** *steken* (to stick, to stab, to pierce, to insert).
*   **Etymology:** From Proto-Germanic **\*stekaną*. It is cognate with English *stick* and German *stechen*.
*   **Semantic Function:** The core meaning involves a sharp, penetrating action or the act of placing something into something else.
*   **Verb Class:** *Steken* is a **strong verb** (*sterk werkwoord*). This means it undergoes vowel gradation (ablaut) in the past tense (*stak*) and past participle (*gestoken*). This irregularity carries over to the compound *afsteken*.

#### C. The Suffix: `-en`
*   **Type:** Inflectional Suffix.
*   **Function:** This is the standard marker for the **infinitive** form of Dutch verbs.
*   **Grammar:** It does not carry specific semantic meaning on its own but signals that the word is the dictionary form of the verb.

---

### 3. Semantic Composition (Meaning)
When combining these morphemes, the meaning is not always purely additive. The prefix `af-` shifts the meaning of the root `steken` in specific idiomatic ways:

1.  **To Light (Fire/Candle):**
    *   *Logic:* To "stick" a flame onto something (ignite).
    *   *Example:* "Een kaars **afsteken**" (To light a candle).
2.  **To Stand Out / Contrast:**
    *   *Logic:* To "stick out" away from a background.
    *   *Example:* "Hij **steekt** er **af**" (He stands out / contrasts with the others).
3.  **To Cut Off:**
    *   *Logic:* To pierce/stick a knife and remove a piece.
    *   *Example:* "Een stuk brood **afsteken**" (To cut off a piece of bread).
4.  **To Bribe (Slang):**
    *   *Logic:* To "pay off" (money sticking away from you to someone else).
    *   *Example:* "Iemand **afsteken**" (To bribe someone).

### 4. Morphosyntactic Features
Because *afsteken* is a compound of a prefix and a strong verb, it follows specific morphological rules:

*   **Past Participle:** The prefix `af-` is separated by the participle marker `ge-`.
    *   *Structure:* `af` + `ge` + `stek` + `en`
    *   *Result:* **`afgestoken`**
*   **Imperative:** The prefix separates.
    *   *Result:* **`Steek af!`**
*   **Noun Derivation:** It can be nominalized.
    *   *Result:* **`het afsteken`** (the lighting/cutting/contrasting).

### Summary
Linguistically, **`afsteken`** is a **separable compound verb**. It combines the directional prefix **`af-`** (off/away) with the strong verbal root **`stek-`** (to stick/pierce) and the infinitive suffix **`-en`**. The combination creates a polysemous verb where the physical action of "piercing" evolves metaphorically into "lighting," "contrasting," or "cutting."
