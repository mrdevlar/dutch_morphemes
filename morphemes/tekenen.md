From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`tekenen`** can be analyzed through **morphology** (the study of word structure). It is a verb in its infinitive form.

Here is the breakdown of the morphemes:

### 1. Segmentation
The word consists of two morphemes:
> **`teken`** + **`-en`**

---

### 2. Morpheme Analysis

#### A. The Root/Stem: `teken`
*   **Type:** Free Morpheme (Lexical Root)
*   **Original Category:** Noun
*   **Meaning:** "Sign," "mark," "symbol," or "signal."
*   **Linguistic Function:** This is the core semantic unit of the word. In Dutch, this root exists independently as a noun (e.g., *een teken geven* — "to give a sign").
*   **Derivational Process:** In the context of the verb *tekenen*, this root undergoes **conversion** (also known as zero-derivation). The noun *teken* is converted into a verb without adding a derivational suffix.
    *   *Semantic Shift:* The meaning shifts from the object ("a sign") to the action associated with it ("to make a sign," "to draw," or "to sign").

#### B. The Suffix: `-en`
*   **Type:** Bound Morpheme (Inflectional Suffix)
*   **Category:** Infinitive Marker
*   **Meaning:** None (Grammatical function only).
*   **Linguistic Function:** This is the standard ending for the **infinitive** form of regular Dutch verbs. It indicates that the verb is in its base form, not conjugated for person or tense.
    *   *Examples:* *lopen* (to walk), *werken* (to work), *maken* (to make).
*   **Note on Ambiguity:** In Dutch, *-en* can also be a plural marker for nouns (e.g., *hond* → *honden*). However, the plural of *teken* is typically *tekens*. Therefore, in the word *tekenen*, *-en* is unambiguously the verbal infinitive marker.

### 4. Syntactic and Semantic Context
*   **Word Class:** Verb (Infinitive).
*   **Primary Meanings:**
    1.  **To draw:** Creating a visual representation (e.g., *Ik teken een huis* — "I am drawing a house").
    2.  **To sign:** Writing one's signature (e.g., *Het contract tekenen* — "To sign the contract").
*   **Conjugation:** When conjugated, the suffix *-en* is removed to form the stem, which is then modified for tense and person.
    *   *Ik teken* (I draw) — Stem only.
    *   *Jij tekent* (You draw) — Stem + *-t*.
    *   *Wij tekenen* (We draw) — Stem + *-en*.

### Conclusion
Linguistically, **`tekenen`** is a **denominal verb** (a verb derived from a noun). It combines the nominal root **`teken`** (sign) with the verbal infinitive suffix **`-en`** to create the action "to draw" or "to sign."
