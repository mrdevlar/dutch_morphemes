From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`alternatief`** is a loanword derived from Latin, adapted into Dutch morphology. Below is a detailed breakdown of its morphemes, analyzed from both a **synchronic** (modern Dutch structure) and **diachronic** (historical/etymological) perspective.

### 1. Word Overview
*   **Word:** `alternatief`
*   **Language:** Dutch
*   **Part of Speech:** Adjective (e.g., *een alternatief plan*) and Noun (e.g., *een alternatief zoeken*).
*   **Meaning:** Alternative; offering a choice between two or more things.
*   **Pronunciation:** /ˌɑl.tɛrˈnaː.tif/

---

### 2. Synchronic Morphological Analysis (Modern Dutch)
In modern Dutch morphology, speakers generally perceive the word as consisting of a **root** and a **derivational suffix**.

| Morpheme | Segment | Type | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Root** | `altern-` | Bound Root | Carries the core lexical meaning (relating to "other" or "switching"). |
| **Suffix** | `-atief` | Derivational | Converts the root into an adjective (or noun) indicating "pertaining to" or "characterized by." |

*   **Note on the Suffix:** In Dutch, the suffix **`-atief`** (often realized as `-ief` in other words like *positief*) is a learned suffix borrowed from Latin *-ativus*. It is used to form adjectives from verbs or nouns.

### 3. Diachronic Morphological Analysis (Etymology)
To understand the internal structure of the root `altern-`, we must look at the Latin origin. The word comes from the Latin **`alternativus`**, which is built from the following layers:

| Morpheme | Latin Origin | Meaning | Evolution to Dutch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Root** | `alter` | "The other (of two)" | Becomes `alter-` in Dutch. |
| **Suffix** | `-n-` | (Adjectival suffix) | Creates *alternus* ("every other"). In Dutch, this `n` is fossilized into the stem `altern-`. |
| **Suffix** | `-ativus` | "Pertaining to / capable of" | Becomes `-atief` in Dutch. |

**Historical Construction:**
`alter` (other) + `-n-` (suffix) + `-ativus` (suffix) $\rightarrow$ *alternativus* $\rightarrow$ *alternatief*.

### Summary
From a linguistic standpoint, **`alternatief`** is a **derived word**.
1.  **Synchronically:** It is composed of the bound root **`altern-`** and the adjectival suffix **`-atief`**.
2.  **Diachronically:** It preserves the Latin structure of **`alter`** (other) + **`-n-`** + **`-ativus`** (pertaining to).
3.  **Functionally:** It serves as both an adjective and a noun in modern Dutch, capable of taking inflectional endings like `-e` (adjective agreement) or `-en` (plural).
