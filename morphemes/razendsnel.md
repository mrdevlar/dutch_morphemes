#### B. The Suffix: `-d`

*   **Grammatical Role:** When used attributively (before a noun) or predicatively, the participle functions as an **adjective**.
*   **Literal Meaning:** "Raging" or "Rushing."

#### C. The Head: `snel`
*   **Meaning:** Fast, quick.
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Old Dutch *snel*, Proto-Germanic *\*snellaz*. It is cognate with the English dialectal word **"snell"** (sharp/quick) and the German **"schnell."**
*   **Grammatical Role:** This is the **head** of the compound. In Dutch compounds, the second element usually determines the grammatical category and the core meaning. Therefore, *razendsnel* is an adjective describing speed.

*   **Literal Interpretation:** "Raging fast."
*   **Actual Meaning:** "Extremely fast," "lightning-fast," or "incredibly quick."
*   **Mechanism:** The word *razend* has undergone **semantic bleaching** regarding its literal meaning of "fury." It no longer implies anger; instead, it functions purely as an **intensifier** (similar to how "dead" in "dead tired" or "stone" in "stone cold" works).
