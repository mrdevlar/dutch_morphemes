From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`taakwisseling`** is a complex word formed through both **derivation** and **compounding**.

Here is the morphological breakdown and explanation of the morphemes.

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented into three distinct morphemes:

> **`taak`** + **`wissel`** + **`-ing`**

Alternatively, from a structural hierarchy perspective, it is often analyzed as a compound of two larger units:
> **`taak`** + **`wisseling`**
> *(Where `wisseling` is itself composed of `wissel` + `-ing`)*

### 2. Detailed Morpheme Analysis

#### A. `taak` (Root / Free Morpheme)
*   **Category:** Noun (Substantive).
*   **Status:** Free morpheme (can stand alone as a word).
*   **Meaning:** "Task," "duty," or "assignment."
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Middle Dutch *taec*, related to the verb *taken* (to take/undertake).
*   **Function in Compound:** It acts as the **modifier** (or dependent). It specifies the *type* of switching that is occurring.

#### B. `wissel` (Stem / Bound Morpheme in this context)
*   **Category:** Verb Stem (derived from the verb *wisselen*).
*   **Status:** While *wissel* can function as a free noun (meaning "switch" or "lever," e.g., *de spoorwegwissel*), in this specific word formation, it functions as the **verbal root**.
*   **Meaning:** "To switch," "to change," or "to exchange."
*   **Function:** It provides the core action of the word.

#### C. `-ing` (Derivational Suffix)
*   **Category:** Suffix (Affix).
*   **Status:** Bound morpheme (cannot stand alone).
*   **Function:** **Nominalizer**. It converts the verb stem (*wissel*) into a noun (*wisseling*).
*   **Meaning:** Indicates "the act or process of [verb]ing."
*   **Grammatical Effect:** It creates a common gender noun (*de wisseling*). It also dictates the plural form (*wisseling**en***).

### 3. Morphological Process

The formation of this word involves two distinct linguistic processes occurring in a specific order:

1.  **Derivation:**
    First, the verb *wisselen* (to switch) is transformed into a noun via the suffix *-ing*.
    *   *wisselen* (Verb) $\rightarrow$ *wisseling* (Noun: "the switching/exchange").

2.  **Compounding:**
    Next, the noun *taak* is combined with the derived noun *wisseling* to form a compound noun.
    *   *taak* + *wisseling* $\rightarrow$ *taakwisseling*.

### 4. Syntactic and Semantic Relationship
*   **Endocentric Compound:** This is an endocentric compound, meaning the meaning of the whole word is a specific type of the head word.
    *   **Head:** *wisseling* (The core concept is a "switching").
    *   **Modifier:** *taak* (It specifies *what* is being switched).
*   **Meaning:** "Task switching" or "exchange of tasks." It refers to the act of swapping duties or roles between people or systems.
