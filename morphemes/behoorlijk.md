From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`behoorlijk`** is a complex word formed through derivational morphology. It consists of three distinct morphemes.

Here is the breakdown:

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented as follows:
> **be-** + **hoor** + **-lijk**

*   **be-**: Prefix
*   **hoor**: Root (Stem of the verb *horen*)
*   **-lijk**: Suffix

---

### 2. Detailed Analysis of Morphemes

#### A. The Prefix: `be-`
*   **Type:** Derivational Prefix (Verbal).
*   **Function:** In Dutch, the prefix *be-* is often used to create transitive verbs from nouns or intransitive verbs. It can also indicate a change of state or a perfective aspect (completing an action).
*   **Etymology:** Cognate with the English prefix *be-* (as in *befriend*, *besmirch*) and the German *be-*. It originates from the Proto-Germanic *\*bi-*.
*   **Role in this word:** It attaches to the root *horen* to form the verb *behoren*. Historically, this shifted the meaning from the physical act of hearing to a more abstract sense of "being heard of" or "belonging."

#### B. The Root: `hoor`
*   **Type:** Bound Root (Stem).
*   **Source:** Derived from the infinitive verb **`horen`** (to hear).
*   **Form:** In Dutch morphology, when adding suffixes to verbs, the infinitive ending *-en* is dropped. Thus, *horen* becomes the stem *hoor-*.
*   **Semantic Shift:** This is the most interesting part of the word.
    *   **Original Meaning:** "To hear."
    *   **Derived Verb (*behoren*):** Over time, *behoren* evolved to mean "to belong" (to be heard of as part of a group) or "to be proper/fitting" (to be heard as correct).
    *   **Current Meaning:** In *behoorlijk*, the root carries the meaning of the verb *behoren*: "to be proper," "to be fitting," or "to belong."

#### C. The Suffix: `-lijk`
*   **Type:** Derivational Suffix (Adjectival/Adverbial).
*   **Function:** It turns a verb or noun into an adjective (and often an adverb). It denotes "characterized by," "worthy of," or "having the nature of."
*   **Etymology:** Cognate with the English suffix **-ly** (as in *friend-ly*, *man-ly*) and the German *-lich*. It comes from the Proto-Germanic *\*-līkijaz* (body, form, shape).
*   **Role in this word:** It transforms the verb *behoren* into an adjective/adverb.
    *   *Behoren* (verb) $\rightarrow$ *Behoorlijk* (adjective/adverb).
    *   Literally: "Characterized by belonging/properness."

---

### 3. Semantic Evolution (Meaning Development)
To understand the modern meaning of *behoorlijk*, one must trace the semantic path of the morphemes:

1.  **Literal Construction:** "Hearing-like" or "Belonging-like."
2.  **Intermediate Meaning:** "Proper" or "Decent."
    *   If something is *behoorlijk*, it fits the rules; it is "proper."
    *   *Example:* *Een behoorlijk mens* (A decent/proper person).
3.  **Modern Usage (Intensifier):** "Quite" or "Considerably."
    *   In contemporary Dutch, the meaning weakened from "proper" to "to a proper degree," and finally to a general intensifier.
    *   *Example:* *Het is behoorlijk koud* (It is **quite** cold).

### 4. English Cognates and Comparisons
Linguistically, this word offers a fascinating parallel to English:

| Dutch Morpheme | English Cognate | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **be-** | **be-** | *be*friend, *be*head |
| **hoor** (from *horen*) | **hear** | *hear*, *hearing* |
| **-lijk** | **-ly** | friend*ly*, man*ly* |

**Comparison:**
*   **Dutch:** *Behoorlijk* (Proper/Quite)
*   **English:** *Hear* + *-ly* does not exist. However, the concept of "proper" is closer to the English word **decent** or **fitting**.
*   **Note:** While the suffix *-lijk* corresponds to *-ly*, the root *hoor* corresponds to *hear*. However, the semantic shift in Dutch (*hear* $\rightarrow$ *belong/proper*) did not happen in English. In English, *hear* remained physical, whereas in Dutch, *horen* acquired the abstract sense of "belonging" (similar to the English phrase "to be heard of").

### Summary
**`Behoorlijk`** is a morphologically transparent word in Dutch. It is built from the prefix **`be-`**, the stem of the verb **`horen`** (`hoor`), and the adjectival suffix **`-lijk`**. While it literally suggests "characterized by belonging/properness," modern usage has grammaticalized it into a common adverb meaning "quite" or "considerably."
