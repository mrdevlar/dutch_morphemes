*   **Part of Speech:** Noun (Substantive)
*   **Function:** It carries the core semantic load of the word.
*   **Meaning:** "Ground," "soil," "earth," or metaphorically "basis/reason."

Unlike words such as *`on-mogelijk`* (impossible: `on-` + `mogelijk`) or *`werk-zaam`* (active: `werk` + `-zaam`), `grond` has no separable affixes. The final `-d` is not a suffix in modern Dutch; it is an integral part of the root stem.

From a semantic perspective, the single morpheme `grond` covers a polysemous range of meanings:

3.  **Property:** Land or real estate (e.g., *een stuk grond* = a piece of land).
