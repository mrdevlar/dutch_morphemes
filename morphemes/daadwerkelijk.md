From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`daadwerkelijk`** is a complex word formed through compounding and derivation. It functions primarily as an **adverb** (meaning "actually," "really," or "in fact") but can also function as an **adjective**.

Here is the morphological breakdown and explanation of the word.

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented into three ultimate morphemes (the smallest units of meaning):

> **`daad`** + **`werk`** + **`-lijk`**
> *(with an epenthetic linking vowel `e`)*

Visually, the structure is often analyzed as a compound of the noun `daad` and the adjective `werkelijk`:
> **[ daad ] + [ werk + elijk ]**

### 2. Detailed Morpheme Analysis

#### A. `daad` (Free Morpheme / Noun)
*   **Meaning:** Deed, act, action.
*   **Function:** In this compound, it acts as a modifier specifying the *domain* of reality. It contrasts "action" with "theory" or "words."
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Middle Dutch *daet*, Old Dutch *dada*. It is cognate with the English word **deed** and the German word *Tat*.
*   **Root:** Proto-Germanic *\*dōn-* (to do).

#### B. `werk` (Free Morpheme / Noun Stem)
*   **Meaning:** Work, effect, operation.
*   **Function:** In the context of *werkelijk*, the meaning shifts from physical "labor" to "functioning" or "existence." Something that "works" is something that is real or effective.
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Middle Dutch *werck*, Old Dutch *werk*. It is cognate with the English word **work** and the German word *Werk*.
*   **Root:** Proto-Germanic *\*werką*.

#### C. `-lijk` (Bound Morpheme / Derivational Suffix)
*   **Meaning:** -ly, -like, -able.
*   **Function:** This suffix turns the preceding noun stem into an adjective (and subsequently an adverb). It indicates a quality or resemblance.
*   **Spelling Note:** In this specific word, an **epenthetic vowel `e`** is inserted between the stem `werk` and the suffix `-lijk` to facilitate pronunciation (breaking up the consonant cluster).
    *   *Strict Morphology:* `werk` + `e` (linking vowel) + `lijk`.
    *   *Lexicalized Analysis:* Often taught as the suffix `-elijk` in this specific context (similar to *mogelijk*, *nuttig* → *nuttelijk*).
*   **Etymology:** Cognate with the English suffix **-ly** (as in *friend-ly*) and German *-lich*.
*   **Root:** Originally related to the word for "body" (*lichaam*), implying "having the form of," but grammaticalized over time to mean "characterized by."

### 3. Semantic Composition (How the meaning is built)

To understand the full meaning, we must look at how the parts combine semantically:

1.  **`werk` + `-lijk` = `werkelijk`**
    *   Literal meaning: "Work-like" or "Effective."
    *   Actual meaning: **Real**, **actual**, **genuine**. (Something that "works" in reality, not just in theory).
2.  **`daad` + `werkelijk` = `daadwerkelijk`**
    *   Literal meaning: "Deed-real-like."
    *   Actual meaning: **Actually**, **in fact**, **de facto**.
    *   **Nuance:** The addition of `daad` emphasizes that the reality is proven through **action** or **practice**, rather than just intention or theory. It distinguishes between something that is *theoretically* true versus something that is *practically* true.

### 4. Syntactic and Phonological Features

*   **Part of Speech:** Adverb (*bijwoord*) or Adjective (*bijvoeglijk naamwoord*).
    *   *Adverb:* "Het is **daadwerkelijk** waar." (It is **actually** true.)
    *   *Adjective:* "Een **daadwerkelijke** oplossing." (An **actual** solution.)
*   **Stress Pattern:** The primary stress falls on the second component: *daad-**WER**-ke-lijk*. This confirms that `werkelijk` is the core unit modified by `daad`.
*   **Pronunciation:** /daːtˈʋɛr.kə.lə(k)/
    *   Note the devoicing of the `d` in `daad` to a `t` sound before the `w` (sandhi).

### Summary
The word **`daadwerkelijk`** is a morphological compound that literally translates to **"deed-work-like."** It combines the concept of action (`daad`) with the concept of effective reality (`werkelijk`) to create a word that emphasizes **practical, proven reality** as opposed to theoretical possibility.
