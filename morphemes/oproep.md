From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`oproep`** can be analyzed as a **deverbal noun** (a noun derived from a verb). It consists of two distinct morphemes.

Here is the breakdown and explanation:

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word is segmented as follows:
> **`op-`** + **`roep`**

### 2. Morpheme Analysis

#### **Morpheme 1: `op-`**
*   **Type:** Derivational Prefix (specifically, a separable verbal prefix).
*   **Origin:** Preposition/Adverb.
*   **Meaning:** Literally "up" or "on."
*   **Function:** In the context of verbs, this prefix adds a directional or completive aspect. When attached to *roepen* (to call), it changes the meaning from a general "calling" to "calling **out**," "summoning," or "appealing to."
*   **Linguistic Note:** In Dutch grammar, `op` is a **separable prefix** (*scheidbaar voorvoegsel*). In a main clause verb conjugation, it separates from the root (e.g., *Ik **roep** je **op*** — "I call you up"). However, in the nominalized form *oproep*, the prefix remains attached to the stem.

#### **Morpheme 2: `roep`**
*   **Type:** Verbal Root / Stem.
*   **Origin:** Proto-Germanic *\*hrēpaną* (to call/shout).
*   **Meaning:** "Call," "shout," or "cry."
*   **Form:** This is the stem of the infinitive verb **`roepen`** (to call).
*   **Function:** It carries the core lexical meaning of the word. In the infinitive *roepen*, the suffix *-en* marks the verb. In the noun *oproep*, the verbal suffix *-en* is dropped, leaving the bare stem.

### 3. Morphological Process: Nominalization
The formation of *oproep* involves **zero-derivation** (also known as conversion).

1.  **Base Verb:** *oproepen* (to call out / to summon).
2.  **Process:** The verbal stem is converted into a noun without adding a specific nominalizing suffix (like *-ing* or *-te*).
3.  **Result:** *oproep* (a call / a summons / an appeal).

*Note: While Dutch often uses the suffix *-ing* to create nouns from verbs (e.g., *oproeping*), the form *oproep* is the standard, lexicalized noun for a "call" or "summons" (e.g., *een oproep tot actie* — a call to action).*
