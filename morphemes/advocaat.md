*   **Origin:** Latin *advocatus* (via Old French *avocat*).

    *   **Origin:** Latin *ad-*
    *   **Meaning:** "to," "towards," or "at."
    *   **Function:** Directional prefix.
2.  **`voc-`** (Root)
    *   **Origin:** Latin *vocare*
    *   **Meaning:** "to call."
    *   **Cognates:** English *vocal*, *voice*, *invoke*.
3.  **`-aat`** (Suffix)
    *   **Origin:** Latin *-atus* (past participle ending) $\rightarrow$ Old French *-at* $\rightarrow$ Dutch *-aat*.
    *   **Meaning:** Indicates an agent or a state of being (one who has been called).
    *   **Function:** Nominalizer (turns the verb concept into a noun).

**Semantic Shift:**
Originally, an *advocatus* was simply someone "called to" assist. In Roman law, this referred to a person summoned to speak in court on behalf of a defendant. Over time, the meaning narrowed specifically to the legal profession.
