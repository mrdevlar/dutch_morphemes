From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`persoonlijk`** (meaning "personal") is a classic example of **derivational morphology**. It is formed by combining a lexical root with a derivational suffix to change the part of speech and refine the meaning.

Here is the breakdown of the morphemes:

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented into two distinct morphemes:

> **`persoon`** + **`-lijk`**

---

### 2. Analysis of Morphemes

#### A. The Root: `persoon`
*   **Type:** Free morpheme (Lexeme).
*   **Part of Speech:** Noun (*Zelfstandig naamwoord*).
*   **Meaning:** "Person," "human being," or "individual."
*   **Etymology:** This is a loanword that entered Dutch from Middle French (*personne*), which in turn derived from the Latin *persona* (originally meaning "mask" or "character" in a play, later "human being").
*   **Linguistic Note:** Although etymologically Latin (*per-sona*), in modern Dutch morphology, it is treated as an **indivisible root**. Dutch speakers do not analyze *per-* and *-soon* separately; they perceive *persoon* as a single unit of meaning.

#### B. The Suffix: `-lijk`
*   **Type:** Bound morpheme (Derivational suffix).
*   **Function:** Adjectivalizer. It converts a noun (or sometimes a verb) into an adjective.
*   **Meaning:** It conveys the sense of "having the quality of," "characterized by," or "relating to."
*   **Cognates:** This is a Germanic suffix. It is directly cognate with:
    *   **English:** *-ly* (e.g., *friend*ly*, *man*ly*, *person*ally*).
    *   **German:** *-lich* (e.g., *mensch*lich*, *freund*lich*).
*   **Phonology:** In Dutch, the addition of *-lijk* typically does not shift the primary stress of the word. The stress remains on the root syllable: *per-**soon**-lijk*.

---

### 3. Semantic Composition (Meaning Construction)
The process is **derivational**, meaning it creates a new word with a new meaning from an existing one.

1.  **Base Meaning:** *Persoon* = A human individual.
2.  **Suffix Application:** *-lijk* = "Relating to" or "Characteristic of."
3.  **Resulting Meaning:** *Persoonlijk* = "Relating to a specific individual" or "Private."

**Comparison with English:**
*   **Dutch:** Uses a Germanic construction (*persoon* + *-lijk*).
*   **English:** Uses a Latinate construction for the adjective (*person* + *-al* → *personal*), though it uses the Germanic suffix for the adverb (*personal* + *-ly* → *personally*).
*   **Note:** Dutch uses *persoonlijk* for both the adjective (*een persoonlijke opmerking* / a personal remark) and the adverb (*ik heb het persoonlijk gezegd* / I said it personally).

### 4. Summary Table

| Morpheme | Type | Origin | Function | Meaning Contribution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **persoon** | Root (Noun) | Latin (via French) | Semantic Base | "Person" / "Individual" |
| **-lijk** | Suffix | Proto-Germanic | Derivational | "Relating to" / "Like" |
| **persoonlijk** | **Derived Word** | **Dutch** | **Adjective** | **"Personal"** |

### 5. Orthographic Note
In Dutch spelling, when adding *-lijk* to a root ending in a vowel (like *persoon*), the spelling of the root remains unchanged. However, if the root were a verb stem ending in a vowel, vowel lengthening rules might apply. In this specific case, the double 'o' in *persoon* is retained to maintain the long vowel sound before the suffix is added.
