From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`handvol`** is a **compound noun** formed through the combination of two free morphemes.

Here is the morphological breakdown and analysis:

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented into two distinct morphemes:

> **hand** + **vol**

### 2. Analysis of Morphemes

#### Morpheme 1: `hand`
*   **Category:** Noun (Substantive).
*   **Meaning:** The body part at the end of the arm used for grasping.
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Proto-Germanic **\*handuz**, which traces back to the Proto-Indo-European root **\*ḱnd-** (to grasp).
*   **Cognates:** English *hand*, German *Hand*, Swedish *hand*.
*   **Function in Compound:** It acts as the **modifier**. It specifies the *capacity* or the *instrument* used to measure the quantity.

#### Morpheme 2: `vol`
*   **Category:** Adjective (functioning nominally in this context).
*   **Meaning:** Full.
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Proto-Germanic **\*fullaz**, tracing back to the Proto-Indo-European root **\*plh₁-** (to fill).
*   **Cognates:** English *full*, German *voll*, Swedish *full*.
*   **Function in Compound:** It acts as the **head** of the compound in terms of semantic category (indicating a state of fullness/quantity), though the entire compound functions as a noun.
    *   *Linguistic Note:* In English, the equivalent word is "hand**ful**." In English, *-ful* is a **derivational suffix** (a bound morpheme). In Dutch, *vol* is a **free morpheme** (it can stand alone as a word: *De beker is vol* / "The cup is full"). This is a key structural difference between the two languages.

### 3. Compound Structure and Semantics
*   **Type:** This is a **Noun + Adjective** compound that results in a **Noun**.
*   **Semantic Composition:** The meaning is compositional but idiomatic. It does not mean "a hand that is full," but rather "an amount of something that fills a hand."
*   **Head Direction:** In Dutch compounds, the rightmost element is typically the semantic head (determining the grammatical category). Here, *vol* determines that the concept is about "fullness/quantity," but the whole word is lexicalized as a unit of measurement.

*   **Singular:** *een handvol* (a handful)
*   **Plural:** *twee handenvol* (two handfuls)

**Analysis:**
