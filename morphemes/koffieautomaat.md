From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`koffieautomaat`** is a **compound noun** (*samengesteld zelfstandig naamwoord*).

Here is the morphological breakdown and explanation of its components.

### 1. Segmentation
The word can be segmented into two distinct free morphemes:

> **`koffie`** + **`automaat`**

There are no inflectional morphemes (such as plural markers or tense markers) in this specific form; it is the singular, uninflected base form.

### 2. Morpheme Analysis

#### A. Morpheme 1: `koffie`
*   **Type:** Free morpheme (can stand alone as a word).
*   **Part of Speech:** Noun.
*   **Meaning:** Coffee.
*   **Etymology:** This is a loanword. It entered Dutch via French (*café*), which derived from Turkish (*kahve*), which ultimately comes from Arabic (*qahwa*).
*   **Phonology:** In Dutch, it is pronounced /ˈkɔfi/.
*   **Morphological Status:** It functions as the **modifier** (or dependent) in this compound. It specifies the *type* or *purpose* of the machine.

#### B. Morpheme 2: `automaat`
*   **Type:** Free morpheme (can stand alone as a word).
*   **Part of Speech:** Noun.
*   **Meaning:** Machine, vending machine, or automaton.
*   **Etymology:** This is a loanword from Latin (*automatum*), derived from Greek (*automatos*, meaning "self-moving").
*   **Internal Structure (Diachronic vs. Synchronic):**
    *   *Diachronically (historically):* It contains the root *auto-* (self) and a suffix related to movement/action.
    *   *Synchronically (modern usage):* In modern Dutch, **`automaat`** is treated as a single, unbreakable morpheme. A speaker does not analyze it as *auto* + *maat* (where *maat* would imply "measure" or "mate"). Therefore, for the purpose of analyzing this specific word, it is one morpheme.
*   **Morphological Status:** It functions as the **head** (or nucleus) of the compound. The entire word refers to a type of *automaat*.

### 3. Compound Structure & Semantics

*   **Compound Type:** **Endocentric**.
    *   In an endocentric compound, the meaning of the whole word is a hyponym (a specific type) of the head.
    *   A `koffieautomaat` is a specific type of `automaat`.
*   **Head-Modifier Relationship:**
    *   **Head:** `automaat` (The core category).
    *   **Modifier:** `koffie` (The specific attribute).
    *   **Semantic Relation:** Purpose/Content. (A machine *for* coffee).
*   **Linking Morpheme:**
    *   Dutch compounds often use a linking element (Fugue), such as `-s-` (e.g., *koffieshop*) or `-en-` (e.g., *bloemenwinkel*).
    *   In `koffieautomaat`, there is **no linking morpheme**. The two nouns are concatenated directly. This is common when the first element ends in a vowel and the second begins with a vowel, though not a strict rule.

### 4. Prosody (Stress)

*   This distinguishes it from a phrase where the stress might fall on the head (e.g., *een automaat voor koffie*).
