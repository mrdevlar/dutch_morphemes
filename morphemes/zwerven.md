*   **Meaning:** It denotes the action of moving without a fixed course, wandering, roaming, or drifting.

*   **Grammatical Category:** Verb stem.
*   **Note on Stem Variation:** In Dutch morphology, this is a **strong verb** (sterk werkwoord). While the infinitive stem is `zwerv-`, the stem changes during conjugation due to **ablaut** (vowel gradation).

#### B. The Suffix: `-en`
*   **Type:** Bound morpheme (inflectional suffix).
*   **Function:** Marks the **infinitive** form of the verb.
*   **Meaning:** It has no lexical meaning of its own; it purely signals grammatical function (non-finite verb form).
*   **Productivity:** This is the standard, productive suffix for the infinitive in Dutch (e.g., *lopen*, *werken*, *zien* → *zien* is irregular, but *zien* is historically *zien-en* with elision).

*   **Proto-Germanic:** Reconstructed as **`*swerbaną`** (to wander, to drift, to turn).

*   **Middle Dutch:** `swerven`.
*   **Cognates:**
