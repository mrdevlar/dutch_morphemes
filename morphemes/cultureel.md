From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`cultureel`** (meaning "cultural") can be analyzed using **derivational morphology**. It is a compound of a root noun and a derivational suffix.

Here is the breakdown of the morphemes:

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented as follows:

> **`cultuur`** + **`-eel`**

*   **`cultuur`**: The root (base noun).
*   **`-eel`**: The derivational suffix.

---

### 2. Detailed Analysis of Morphemes

#### A. The Root: `cultuur`
*   **Type:** Free morpheme (Noun).
*   **Meaning:** "Culture," "cultivation," or "refinement."
*   **Etymology:** Derived from the Latin *cultura* (tilling, cultivation), which entered Dutch via French (*culture*).
*   **Function:** It provides the core semantic meaning of the word.
*   **Note on Allomorphy:** In standard Dutch, the noun is spelled *cultuur*. However, when forming the adjective, the stem undergoes a slight change to **`culture-`**. This is known as **stem allomorphy**. The word does not become *cultuureel*; instead, it adopts the international stem form *culture-* to accommodate the suffix.

#### B. The Suffix: `-eel`
*   **Type:** Bound morpheme (Derivational Suffix).
*   **Function:** It changes the part of speech from a **Noun** to an **Adjective**.
*   **Meaning:** It conveys the meaning "pertaining to," "related to," or "characterized by."
*   **Etymology:** This suffix comes from the Latin adjectival suffix *-alis* (as in *culturalis*), which passed into Dutch via French (*-el*). In Dutch, this is standardized as *-eel* (often spelled with a double 'e' to indicate the long vowel sound /eː/).
*   **Examples:**
    *   *Sociaal* (from *society* / *sociëteit* context)
    *   *Nationaal* (from *natie*)
    *   *Emotioneel* (from *emotie*)

---

### 3. Morphophonological Note (Spelling & Pronunciation)
A key linguistic feature of this word is the interaction between the root and the suffix, specifically regarding the spelling **`cultureel`** versus the noun **`cultuur`**.

1.  **Stem Variation:** If Dutch morphology were perfectly regular based on the noun *cultuur*, one might expect the spelling *cultuureel*. However, because *cultureel* is a "loanword adaptation," it retains the **international stem** (*culture-*) rather than the fully Dutchified noun stem (*cultuur-*).
2.  **Vowel Length:** The double **`ee`** in *-eel* indicates a long vowel sound (/eː/). In Dutch orthography, a single 'e' in an open syllable would be short, so the doubling is necessary to maintain the correct pronunciation.
3.  **Phonology:** The pronunciation is /kyːtyˈreːl/. The stress falls on the final syllable (**-eel**), which is typical for Dutch adjectives ending in this suffix (e.g., *natuurlijk* vs. *cultureel*).

Linguistically, **`cultureel`** is a **derived adjective**. It is formed by taking the noun root **`cultuur`** and attaching the adjectival suffix **`-eel`**. The spelling reflects a blend of the Dutch noun and the international (Latin/French) stem used for derivation.
