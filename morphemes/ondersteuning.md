From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`ondersteuning`** (meaning "support," "backing," or "assistance") is a complex word formed through **derivational morphology**. It is built from three distinct morphemes.

Here is the breakdown and explanation of each component.

### 1. Morphological Segmentation

The word can be segmented as follows:

> **`onder`** + **`steun`** + **`-ing`**

### 2. Detailed Morpheme Analysis

#### A. `onder` (Prefix)
*   **Type:** Inseparable Prefix (in this specific verb context).
*   **Literal Meaning:** "Under," "below," or "beneath."
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Proto-Germanic **\*under**, cognate with English *under* and German *unter*.
*   **Function:** In the verb *ondersteunen* (to support), *onder* acts as a directional prefix. It modifies the root to imply an action coming from below to hold something up.
*   **Linguistic Note:** In Dutch, verbs with the prefix *onder-* are generally **inseparable**. This is indicated by the stress pattern. In *ondersteunen*, the stress falls on the root (**steun**), not the prefix (*on*dersteunen). This distinguishes it from separable verbs (like *op*roepen), where the prefix is stressed.

#### B. `steun` (Root / Lexeme)
*   **Type:** Nominal Root (which serves as the verbal stem).
*   **Meaning:** "Support," "prop," "buttress," or "crutch."
*   **Etymology:** From Middle Dutch *stoen*, related to Old High German *stoum* (stump, support). It is distantly related to the English word *stump* (originally a support or post) and the verb *stay*.
*   **Function:** This is the core semantic carrier of the word. It provides the fundamental concept of physical or metaphorical holding up.
*   **Grammar:** While *steun* exists as a standalone noun (*de steun*), in this construction, it functions as the stem of the verb *steunen* (to lean on/support).

#### C. `-ing` (Suffix)
*   **Type:** Derivational Suffix (Nominalizer).
*   **Meaning:** Indicates the **action**, **process**, or **result** of the verb.
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Proto-Germanic **\*-ungō**, cognate with English *-ing* (as in *building* or *feeling*) and German *-ung* (as in *Unterstützung*).
*   **Function:** This suffix performs **nominalization**. It converts the verb *ondersteunen* (to support) into the abstract noun *ondersteuning* (support/supporting).
*   **Phonology:** When adding *-ing* to a verb stem ending in a vowel or specific consonants, Dutch often requires a linking vowel (like *-e-*). However, since *steun* ends in a nasal consonant, the suffix attaches directly without a linking vowel.

### 3. Derivation Process

To understand the full morphology, it helps to trace the word's creation from the verb:

1.  **Base Root:** `steun` (support/prop)
2.  **Verb Formation:** `onder` + `steun` + `-en` $\rightarrow$ **`ondersteunen`** (to support).
    *   *Note:* The infinitive suffix `-en` marks the verb.
3.  **Noun Formation:** `ondersteun` (verb stem) + `-ing` $\rightarrow$ **`ondersteuning`** (support).
    *   *Note:* The infinitive `-en` is dropped before adding the nominal suffix `-ing`.

### 4. Semantic Composition

The meaning of the whole word is compositional but has undergone semantic broadening:

*   **Literal:** "The act of propping something from underneath."
*   **Figurative:** "Assistance," "financial aid," "emotional backing," or "technical support."

### Summary
**`Ondersteuning`** is a **deverbal noun** created by adding the nominalizing suffix **`-ing`** to the verb stem **`ondersteun-`**. The verb itself is formed by the inseparable prefix **`onder-`** ("under") and the root **`steun`** ("prop"). Together, they linguistically encode the concept of "holding something up from below."
