### 2. Detailed Analysis of Morphemes

#### A. `beweg` (Bound Root)
*   **Type:** Bound Root (Lexeme).
*   **Origin:** Derived from the verb **`bewegen`** (to move).
*   **Status:** In modern Dutch, `beweg` cannot stand alone as a word. It must be inflected (e.g., `beweeg`, `beweegt`) or derivationalized (e.g., `beweging`).
*   **Etymology:** Traces back to Middle Dutch *bewegen* and Proto-Germanic *\*bi-weganą*.
*   **Semantics:** Carries the core meaning of "motion," "movement," or "physical activity."

#### B. `-ing` (Derivational Suffix)
*   **Type:** Nominalizing Suffix.
*   **Function:** This suffix converts the verbal root (`beweg`) into a noun. This process is called **deverbalization**.
*   **Result:** `beweging` (noun).
*   **Meaning:** It denotes the action, process, or result of the verb. Here, it means "movement" or "exercise."
*   **Linguistic Note:** This is a very productive suffix in Dutch (e.g., *lezen* → *lezing*, *werken* → *werking*).

#### C. `-s-` (Interfix / Linking Element)
*   **Type:** Interfix (also known as a *voegsel* or *fugue*).
*   **Function:** It connects the first part of the compound (`beweging`) to the second part (`app`).
*   **Grammar Rule:** In Dutch compounding, an `-s-` is frequently inserted when the first noun ends in specific suffixes, particularly **-ing**, **-heid**, **-teit**, or **-te**.
    *   *Examples:* `rekening` + `blad` = `rekeningsblad`; `liefde` + `brief` = `liefdesbrief`.
*   **Important Distinction:** This `-s-` is **not** a plural marker, nor is it a genitive case marker in the modern sense. It is purely a phonological and morphological linker to ensure the compound flows correctly.

#### D. `app` (Free Morpheme / Loanword)
*   **Type:** Noun (Loanword).
*   **Origin:** English abbreviation for **`application`**.
*   **Status:** A free morpheme (it can stand alone as a word in Dutch: *"Ik heb een app"*).
*   **Integration:** It has been fully nativized into Dutch. It follows Dutch pluralization rules (e.g., `apps` or `appjes`) and capitalization rules (usually lowercase unless starting a sentence).
*   **Semantics:** Refers to a software application, specifically on a mobile device.
