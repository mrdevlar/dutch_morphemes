### Linguistic Note on "Midden" vs. "Mid"
You might notice the English word *mid* (as in *midnight*). In Dutch, the root *mid-* exists but is usually bound (it must attach to another word).
*   **Bound Root:** *mid-* (e.g., *middernacht* = midnight, *middag* = afternoon).
*   **Free Root:** *midden* (e.g., *in het midden* = in the middle).

In the phrase "`midden in`", the word is the free root form **midden**, not the bound root *mid*. This is why it stands alone as a word rather than attaching to *in* to form a compound like *midinin* (which does not exist).
