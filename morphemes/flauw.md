From a strict linguistic (morphological) perspective, the Dutch word **`flauw`** is a **monomorphemic** word. This means it consists of a single, indivisible unit of meaning. It cannot be broken down into smaller meaningful parts (prefixes, suffixes, or compound roots) in modern Standard Dutch.

Here is the detailed linguistic breakdown:

### 1. Synchronic Morphological Analysis (Modern Dutch)
In the current state of the language, `flauw` functions as a **free lexical morpheme**.

*   **Morpheme:** `flauw`
*   **Type:** Root (Free Morpheme)
*   **Category:** Adjective (can function as an adverb)
*   **Meaning:** Faint, weak, insipid, lame (as in a joke), pale.

**Why it cannot be segmented further:**
Unlike words such as *`on-vriendelijk`* (un-friendly) or *`flauw-te`* (faintness), `flauw` contains no derivational affixes (like *on-*, *-te*, *-en*) and is not a compound of two independent words (like *`flauw-vallen`* to faint).

### 3. Diachronic Analysis (Etymology & History)
To understand the internal history of the word, we look at its evolution. While it is one morpheme today, it evolved from older Germanic roots.

*   **Middle Dutch:** *flau*, *flauwe*
*   **Old High German:** *flou*
*   **Proto-Germanic:** Reconstructed as **\*flauwaz**
*   **Cognates:**
    *   German: *flau* (weak, faint, mild)

**Semantic Shift:**
Historically, the root meaning was physical weakness or faintness. Over time, the meaning expanded metaphorically:
1.  **Physical:** Fainting (*flauwvallen*).
2.  **Sensory:** Insipid taste (*een flauwe soep* - a bland soup).
3.  **Intellectual/Humor:** Lame or unfunny (*een flauwe grap* - a lame joke).
4.  **Visual:** Pale color (*flauwe kleuren* - pale colors).

### 4. Derivational Morphology (How it combines)
Although `flauw` itself is a single morpheme, it serves as a **base** (stem) to which other morphemes can be attached to create new words. This proves its status as a root morpheme.

*   **`flauw` + `-te`** $\rightarrow$ **`flauwte`**

    *   *Meaning:* Faintness; a fainting spell.
*   **`flauw` + `vallen`** $\rightarrow$ **`flauwvallen`**
    *   *Morphology:* Adjective + Verb (Compound Verb).

    *   *Morphology:* Prefix + Root + Verbal Suffix.
    *   *Meaning:* To become faint/weak.
