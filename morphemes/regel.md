From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`regel`** is best analyzed as a **monomorphemic root** in its modern synchronic state (how it functions today), though it has a rich diachronic history (etymology).

Here is the breakdown and explanation of the morphemes involved.

### 1. Synchronic Morphological Analysis (Modern Dutch)
In contemporary Dutch, the word **`regel`** consists of a single morpheme.

*   **Morpheme:** `regel`
*   **Type:** **Free Root Morpheme**
*   **Function:** It can stand alone as a word (a noun or a verb stem). It carries the core lexical meaning and cannot be divided into smaller meaningful units within the current language system.

**Why it is not broken down further:**
Unlike a word like *`on+regel+matig`* (irregular), where you have a prefix (*on-*), a root (*regel*), and a suffix (*-matig*), the word *`regel`* by itself contains no affixes.
*   **Inflection:** When pluralized (*`regels`*), the suffix *-s* is added, but the root remains *`regel`*.
*   **Derivation:** When verbalized (*`regelen`*), the infinitive suffix *-en* is added, but the root remains *`regel`*.

### 2. Diachronic Morphological Analysis (Etymology)
While it is one morpheme today, its history reveals a borrowing and semantic shift.

*   **Origin:** Latin **`regula`** (meaning "straight rod," "pattern," or "rule").
*   **Path:** Latin *regula* $\rightarrow$ Old Frankish/Old Dutch *regel*.
*   **Semantic Shift:**
    1.  **Concrete:** Originally referred to a physical straight edge or ruler (a tool for drawing lines).
    2.  **Abstract:** Metonymically shifted to mean a "norm," "law," or "guideline" (something that keeps behavior "straight").
*   **Cognates:** It is related to the verb *`regeren`* (to rule/govern) and the English word *`rule`* (via the same Latin root *regula*), though *`regel`* is the direct noun form in Dutch.

### 3. Polysemy (Multiple Meanings)
Because `regel` is a single root morpheme, it carries a high degree of polysemy (multiple related meanings). The context determines which meaning is active:

1.  **Line/Row:** A line of text or a row in a table (*"Schrijf op de volgende regel"* / Write on the next line).
2.  **Norm/Regulation:** A law or standard of conduct (*"De regels van het spel"* / The rules of the game).
3.  **Verse:** A line of poetry (*"Een regel uit een gedicht"* / A line from a poem).
4.  **Arrangement (Verb Stem):** When used as the stem of the verb *`regelen`*, it implies "to arrange" or "to settle" (*"Ik regel dat"* / I'll arrange that).

### 4. Derivational Potential
Although `regel` is one morpheme, it is a highly productive **base** for creating new words. It frequently combines with other morphemes:

*   **Prefixation:**
    *   `on-` + `regel` + `matig` = **`onregelmatig`** (irregular).
    *   `te-` + `regel` = **`te regelen`** (to be arranged).
*   **Suffixation:**
    *   `regel` + `-en` = **`regelen`** (verb: to regulate/arrange).
    *   `regel` + `-ing` = **`regeling`** (noun: regulation/arrangement).
    *   `regel` + `-s` = **`regels`** (noun: plural).
    *   `regel` + `-achtig` = **`regelachtig`** (adjective: rule-like).

Linguistically, **`regel`** is a **monomorphemic lexical root**.
