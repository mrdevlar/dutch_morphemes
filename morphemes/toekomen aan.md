From a strict linguistic perspective, it is important to clarify a fundamental distinction before breaking down the components: **"`toekomen aan`" is not a single word.**

It is a **prepositional verb construction** consisting of a **separable verb** (*toekomen*) and a **preposition** (*aan*). In Dutch grammar, this is often categorized as a *voorzetselwerkwoord* (prepositional verb) or a complex predicate.

However, treating the unit as requested, here is the morphological and syntactic breakdown of the components.

### 1. Morphological Breakdown of `toekomen`

The verb *toekomen* is a **separable verb** (*scheidbaar werkwoord*). In the infinitive form, it appears as one word, but in a main clause, the prefix separates from the root.

| Morpheme | Type | Function | Etymology/Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`toe-`** | **Prefix** (Derivational) | Directional / Aspectual | Derived from Old Dutch *to*. Indicates movement **towards** a target, closure, or addition. In this context, it modifies the direction of the coming. |
| **`kom-`** | **Root** (Lexical) | Semantic Core | The stem of the verb *komen* (to come). Derived from Proto-Germanic *\*kumaną*. Carries the core meaning of motion/arrival. |
| **`-en`** | **Suffix** (Inflectional) | Infinitive Marker | Marks the verb as the infinitive form. In conjugated forms (e.g., *ik kom*), this suffix is dropped. |

**Combined Meaning of `toekomen`:** Literally "to come towards." However, as a lexicalized unit, it implies "to reach," "to arrive at," or "to get to."

### 2. Morphological Breakdown of `aan`

| Morpheme | Type | Function | Etymology/Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`aan`** | **Free Morpheme** (Preposition) | Case/Relation Marker | A preposition indicating contact, direction, or attribution. Cognate with English *on* and German *an*. |

### 3. Syntactic and Semantic Integration

While `toekomen` and `aan` are separate words, they form a tight semantic unit. In linguistics, this is analyzed as a **complex predicate**.

#### A. Valency and Argument Structure
The combination *toekomen aan* changes the valency of the verb *komen*.
*   **`komen` alone:** Intransitive (Subject + Verb). *Ik kom.* (I come.)
*   **`toekomen aan`:** Requires a prepositional object. *Ik kom **aan** iets/jou **toe**.* (I reach something/you.)

#### B. The "Separable" Nature
Because `toe` is a separable prefix, the word order changes in a sentence. The preposition `aan` remains attached to the object, while `toe` moves to the end of the clause.
*   **Infinitive:** *Ik wil **toekomen aan** de taak.* (I want to get to the task.)
*   **Main Clause:** *Ik **kom** niet **aan** de taak **toe**.* (I do not get to the task.)

#### C. Semantic Nuances
The combination of these morphemes creates three distinct idiomatic meanings depending on context:

1.  **Physical Reach:** To physically arrive at a location or person.
    *   *Example:* "De post komt **aan** het huis **toe**." (The mail reaches the house.)
2.  **Temporal/Opportunity:** To get around to doing something (often used in the negative).
    *   *Example:* "Ik kom er niet **aan toe**." (I don't get around to it / I don't have time for it.)
3.  **Attribution/Entitlement:** To be due to someone (often used with *toe* as an adverb, but closely related).
    *   *Example:* "Dat geld komt **aan** mij **toe**." (That money is due to me / belongs to me.)

### Summary of the Unit

If we were to diagram the morphological structure of the phrase as a single conceptual unit:

1.  **`toe`**: Directional Prefix (Modifies `komen`)
2.  **`kom`**: Lexical Root (Motion)
3.  **`-en`**: Infinitive Suffix
4.  **`aan`**: Prepositional Head (Links the verb to its object)

**Linguistic Classification:**
*   **`toekomen`**: Separable Verb (Compound of Prefix + Root + Suffix).
*   **`aan`**: Preposition.
*   **`toekomen aan`**: Prepositional Verb Phrase (Complex Predicate).

In summary, while written as a phrase, linguistically it functions as a single semantic operator where the prefix `toe-` adds directionality to `komen`, and the preposition `aan` specifies the target of that directionality.
