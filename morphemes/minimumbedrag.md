Here is the morphological breakdown and linguistic explanation of the word.

*   **Orthography:** Closed compound (written as one word, no space or hyphen).
*   **Stress Pattern:** In Dutch compounds, the primary stress typically falls on the first syllable of the first constituent.

*   **Integration:** Although of Latin origin, it is fully integrated into Dutch morphology and can take Dutch plural markers (e.g., *minimums* in other contexts, though here it remains singular as a modifier).

#### B. The Head: `bedrag`
This is a **derived noun** (*afgeleid woord*). It is not a single atomic morpheme but has an internal structure of its own.

*   **Internal Breakdown:** `be-` + `drag` + `-(e)` (fossilized)
*   **Root:** `drag`
    *   **Source:** Old Dutch *dragan* / Proto-Germanic *\*draganą*.
    *   **Meaning:** "To carry" or "to bear."
*   **Prefix:** `be-`
    *   **Type:** Derivational prefix.
    *   **Function:** In Dutch, `be-` often turns an intransitive verb into a transitive one or creates a verb indicating the covering or affecting of an object.
    *   **Verb Form:** `bedragen` (to amount to / to carry a value).
*   **Nominalization:** The suffix is effectively zero or a fossilized participle ending.
    *   Historically, `bedrag` is the past participle of the verb `bedragen` used as a noun (similar to English *the amount* vs. *to amount*).
    *   **Semantic Shift:** The meaning shifted from the physical act of "carrying" (`dragen`) to the abstract concept of "holding a value" (`bedragen`), resulting in the noun "amount" or "sum" (`bedrag`).

### 3. Semantic Composition
The meaning of the compound is compositional, meaning it is derived from the sum of its parts, but with specific semantic narrowing.

*   **`bedrag`** = A sum of money or a quantity.
*   **`minimum`** = The lowest limit.
*   **`minimumbedrag`** = The lowest limit of a sum of money or quantity.

**Linguistic Note on Head-Finality:**
Dutch is a **head-final** language regarding compounds. The semantic head is the *last* element (`bedrag`). Therefore, the word refers to a *bedrag* (amount), not a *minimum* (limit). If the order were reversed (`bedragminimum`), it would imply a "limit of an amount" (which is semantically similar but grammatically distinct in focus).
