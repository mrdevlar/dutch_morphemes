From a morphological perspective, the Dutch word **`opname`** is a **derived noun** formed from a separable verb. It consists of three distinct morphemes.

Here is the breakdown:

### 1. Segmentation
**`op`** + **`nam`** + **`e`**

---

### 2. Morpheme Analysis

#### **A. `op-` (Prefix)**
*   **Type:** Separable verbal prefix (part of a separable verb).
*   **Function:** It modifies the meaning of the root verb. In this context, it adds a directional or completive aspect.
*   **Meaning:** Literally "up" or "on." When combined with *nemen* (to take), it implies "taking up," "taking in," or "recording."
*   **Context:** In the infinitive verb form, this prefix is separated from the stem (e.g., *Ik neem het **op*** = I take it **up** / I record it). In the noun form *opname*, it is fused to the front.

#### **B. `nam-` (Root / Stem)**
*   **Type:** Verbal Root (Bound Root).
*   **Source:** Derived from the strong verb **`nemen`** (to take).
*   **Phonological Note (Ablaut):** This is a crucial linguistic feature. The underlying root of the verb is *nem-* (seen in the present tense *ik neem*). However, in this specific nominalization, the root appears with a vowel change (ablaut) to **`nam-`**. This is characteristic of Dutch strong verbs (Class 3), where the vowel shifts between present (*ee*), past (*a*), and past participle (*o*).
    *   *nemen* (infinitive)
    *   *nam* (past tense)
    *   *opname* (noun uses the past tense vowel grade).
*   **Meaning:** "Take."

#### **C. `-e` (Suffix)**
*   **Type:** Derivational Suffix (Nominalizer).
*   **Function:** It converts the verbal stem into a noun.
*   **Productivity:** This suffix is not highly productive in modern Dutch for all verbs (unlike *-ing* or *-te*), but it is common in specific lexicalized nouns derived from strong verbs (e.g., *opname*, *inname*, *aanneming* uses *-ing*, showing variation).
*   **Meaning:** Indicates the action or result of the verb.

---

### 3. Semantic Composition (How the meaning is built)

1.  **`nemen`** = To take.
2.  **`opnemen`** = To take up / To record / To admit (to a hospital).
3.  **`opname`** = The act or result of taking up.

Depending on the context, the meaning shifts:
*   **Media:** A recording (taking up sound/video).
*   **Medical:** Admission (taking a patient into a hospital).
*   **General:** Uptake (taking up a substance or position).

| Morpheme | Type | Origin | Meaning |

| **op-** | Prefix | Separable Verb Particle | Up / On / In |

### 5. Important Linguistic Note
Do not confuse the segment **`name`** with the English word "name." In Dutch, the word for "name" is **`naam`**. The sequence `name` in *opname* is purely coincidental to English speakers; it is the result of the verb stem *nam* + the suffix *e*.
