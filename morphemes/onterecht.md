From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`onterecht`** is a complex word formed through derivational morphology. It is primarily used as an adverb (meaning "unjustly" or "wrongly") or an adjective (meaning "unjust").

Here is the morphological breakdown and explanation of its components.

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented into three distinct layers of morphemes:

> **on-** + **te-** + **recht**

However, in modern synchronic analysis (how the word is processed today), it is often viewed as a prefix attached to a lexicalized base:

> **on-** + **terecht**

---

### 2. Detailed Morpheme Analysis

#### A. The Prefix: `on-`
*   **Type:** Derivational Prefix (Negative/Privative).
*   **Function:** Negation. It reverses the meaning of the base word.
*   **Etymology:** Cognate with the English prefix *un-* (as in *unjust*) and the German *un-*.
*   **Meaning:** "Not," "Without," or "Lacking."
*   **Linguistic Note:** In Dutch, *on-* is the most common negative prefix for adjectives and adverbs. It attaches to the base *terecht* to create the opposite meaning.

#### B. The Base: `terecht`
*   **Type:** Lexicalized Adverb/Adjective.
*   **Meaning:** "Rightly," "Correctly," "Justly," or "In the right place."
*   **Internal Structure:** Historically and etymologically, this is a compound of two morphemes:
    1.  **`te-`**: A preposition meaning "to" or "at." In this context, it indicates direction or state.
    2.  **`recht`**: The core root (see below).
*   **Grammaticalization:** Originally a prepositional phrase (*te recht* = "at right"), it fused over time into a single lexical unit (*terecht*). Because it is now a single unit, the prefix *on-* attaches to the whole word rather than just the root *recht*.

#### C. The Root: `recht`
*   **Type:** Free Root (Noun/Adjective).
*   **Meaning:** "Right," "Law," "Straight," or "Correct."
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Proto-Germanic *\*rehtaz*.
*   **Cognates:**
    *   English: *right*
    *   German: *Recht*
    *   Old English: *riht*
*   **Semantic Field:** This root carries the core semantic weight of the word, relating to justice, correctness, and alignment.

---

### 3. Semantic Composition (Meaning Construction)

To understand the full meaning, we can reconstruct the logic of the word:

1.  **`recht`**: The concept of "right" or "law."
2.  **`te` + `recht`**: Literally "to the right" or "at the right place." Semantically, this evolved to mean "in accordance with the law" or "correctly."
3.  **`on` + `terecht`**: "Not correctly" or "Not in accordance with the law."

**Result:** *Unjustly* or *Wrongly*.

### 4. Comparison with Related Forms
To fully understand the morphology, it helps to compare *onterecht* with its siblings:

*   **`recht`**: Right / Law (Noun/Adj).
*   **`terecht`**: Rightly / Correctly (Adverb).
*   **`onrecht`**: Injustice (Noun). *Note: Here `on-` attaches directly to `recht`.*
*   **`onterecht`**: Unjustly (Adverb) / Unjust (Adj). *Note: Here `on-` attaches to `terecht`.*

### Conclusion
Linguistically, **`onterecht`** is a **negated adverb**. It demonstrates the Dutch tendency to create adverbs by fusing prepositions with nouns (*te* + *recht*) and then applying negative prefixes (*on-*) to those fused units. While the internal structure of *terecht* is visible to etymologists, for a modern speaker, *terecht* functions as a single morphological block to which *on-* is added.
