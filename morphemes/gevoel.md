From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`gevoel`** (meaning "feeling," "sense," or "emotion") is a **derived noun**. It is formed through a process of derivation where affixes are added to a root to change its grammatical category (from verb to noun) and meaning.

Here is the morphological breakdown and explanation.

### 1. Morphological Breakdown

The word consists of **two** primary morphemes:

| Morpheme | Type | Form | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ge-** | Prefix | Derivational | Nominalizer (creates a noun from a verb) |
| **voel** | Root | Lexical | Carries the core semantic meaning ("to feel") |

**Structure:** `ge-` + `voel` = **`gevoel`**

---

### 2. Detailed Analysis of Morphemes

#### A. The Root: `voel`
*   **Origin:** This is the stem of the Dutch verb **`voelen`** (to feel).
*   **Etymology:** It stems from the Old Dutch *fōlen*, which traces back to the Proto-Germanic **\*fōlōną* (to touch, feel).
*   **Meaning:** It carries the core lexical meaning of perception, sensation, or emotion.
*   **Phonology:** In the verb *voelen*, the stem is *voel-* (the *-en* is the infinitive ending). In the noun *gevoel*, the stem remains *voel*.
*   **Note on the 'l':** The letter *l* is part of the root itself, not a separate suffix. This distinguishes it from words where *-el* is a distinct nominalizing suffix (like *tafel* from *tafelen*, though that is irregular). In *gevoel*, the *l* belongs to the concept of "feeling."

#### B. The Prefix: `ge-`
*   **Type:** Derivational Prefix.
*   **Function:** In this specific context, `ge-` acts as a **nominalizer**. It transforms the verbal concept (the action of feeling) into an abstract noun (the state or result of feeling).
*   **Grammatical Gender:** In Dutch, nouns formed with the `ge-` prefix derived from verbs are almost exclusively **neuter** (*het-woorden*). Therefore, the article is *het gevoel*, not *de gevoel*.
*   **Semantic Nuance:**
    *   In verbs, `ge-` usually marks the past participle (e.g., *gevoeld* = felt).
    *   In nouns, `ge-` often indicates a **collective**, an **abstract concept**, or the **result** of the action.
    *   *Example:* *Gebruik* (use) from *gebruiken* (to use); *Geval* (case) from *vallen* (to fall).
    *   In *gevoel*, it abstracts the physical or emotional act of "feeling" into a concrete entity (an emotion or intuition).

### 3. Etymological Context

*   **Proto-Germanic:** **\*ga-fōl-**
    *   The prefix *\*ga-* is the ancestor of the Dutch/German *ge-*. It originally had a collective or completive meaning.
    *   The root *\*fōl-* relates to touching or sensing.
*   **Cognates:**
    *   **German:** *Gefühl* (feeling/sense).
    *   **English:** *Feel* (verb). While English does not use the *ge-* prefix for nouns (it uses *-ing* or *-tion*), the root is identical.
    *   **Frisian:** *Gefoel*.

### 4. Related Derivational Family

To understand the morphology of `gevoel` better, it helps to look at its word family. This shows how the root `voel` interacts with other affixes:

1.  **`voelen`** (Verb): *Root* + Infinitive suffix *-en*. (To feel).
2.  **`gevoel`** (Noun): Prefix *ge-* + *Root*. (The feeling).
3.  **`gevoeld`** (Participle): Prefix *ge-* + *Root* + Suffix *-d*. (Felt).
4.  **`gevoelig`** (Adjective): *Root* + Suffix *-ig* (meaning "full of" or "prone to"). (Sensitive).
5.  **`gevoelloos`** (Adjective): *Noun* + Suffix *-loos* (meaning "without"). (Insensitive/Heartless).

### Summary
Linguistically, **`gevoel`** is a **neuter noun** created by adding the nominalizing prefix **`ge-`** to the verbal root **`voel`**. This morphological process shifts the meaning from the *action* of sensing to the *abstract concept* or *result* of that sensation.
