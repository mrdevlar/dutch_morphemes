### 2. Micro-Segmentation (Morphemic Level)
We can break these two components down further into their constituent morphemes.

1.  **`te`**
    *   **Type:** Preposition (fossilized prefix).
    *   **Meaning:** "to," "at," or "towards."
    *   **Function:** In this context, it indicates direction or destination.
2.  **`recht`**
    *   **Type:** Adjective / Noun Root.
    *   **Meaning:** "right," "straight," or "correct."
    *   **Function:** Indicates correctness or the proper path.
    *   **Combined Meaning (`te` + `recht`):** Literally "to the right place." Over time, this fused into the adverb *terecht*, meaning "correctly," "properly," or "at the destination."

#### B. The Verb: `kunnen`
This is the infinitive form of the modal verb "to can" / "to be able to."

3.  **`kunn`** (or `kun`)
    *   **Type:** Verb Stem (Root).
    *   **Meaning:** The core lexical meaning of ability or possibility.
    *   **Note:** In Dutch orthography, the stem is often doubled (*kunn*) before the suffix *-en* to indicate the short vowel sound (phonetically /ʏ/), distinguishing it from a long vowel.
4.  **`-en`**
    *   **Type:** Inflectional Suffix.
    *   **Function:** Marks the **infinitive** form of the verb.
    *   **Grammar:** In Dutch, the infinitive marker is almost always *-en* (e.g., *lopen*, *zien*, *kunnen*).
