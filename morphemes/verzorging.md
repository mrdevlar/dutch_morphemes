From a morphological perspective, the Dutch word **`verzorging`** is a complex noun derived through a process of **derivational morphology**. It is built from three distinct morphemes.

Here is the breakdown:

### 1. Visual Segmentation
**`ver-`** + **`zorg`** + **`-ing`**

---

### 2. Morpheme Analysis

#### A. The Root: `zorg`
*   **Type:** Free Root / Lexeme
*   **Part of Speech:** Noun (originally), Verb Stem
*   **Meaning:** "Care," "worry," or "concern."
*   **Function:** This is the core semantic unit of the word. It carries the primary meaning.
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Old Dutch *sorga*. It is cognate with the German *Sorge* and related to the English word *sorrow* (via the Proto-Germanic root *\*surgōną*, meaning to worry or be anxious).
*   **Usage:** As a standalone noun, *de zorg* means "the care" or "the worry."

#### B. The Prefix: `ver-`
*   **Type:** Derivational Prefix
*   **Function:** Verbalizer / Semantic Modifier
*   **Meaning:** In this context, `ver-` transforms the root into a transitive verb (*verzorgen*). It often implies:
    1.  **Completion or thoroughness:** Doing something fully.
    2.  **Change of state:** Moving something into a specific condition.
    3.  **Directionality:** Applying the action *to* an object.
*   **Semantic Shift:**
    *   *Zorgen* (verb): To worry, to care (often intransitive or reflexive).
    *   *Verzorgen* (verb): To take care **of** someone/something, to provide for, to maintain.
    *   *Linguistic Note:* The prefix `ver-` is one of the most common verbalizing prefixes in Dutch. It turns the concept of "care/worry" into the active process of "providing care."

#### C. The Suffix: `-ing`
*   **Type:** Derivational Suffix
*   **Function:** Nominalizer
*   **Meaning:** Indicates the **action**, **process**, or **result** of the verb.
*   **Grammar:** It converts the verb stem (*verzorg-*) into a noun (*verzorging*).
*   **Cognate:** This is directly cognate with the English suffix *-ing* (as in *building*, *running*) or the Latin-derived *-tion* (as in *provision*, *formation*).
*   **Gender:** In Dutch, nouns ending in *-ing* are almost exclusively **de-words** (common gender).

---

### 3. Layered Derivation Process
To understand the word fully, it helps to view the derivation in two steps:

1.  **Verb Formation:**
    `zorg` (Root) + `ver-` (Prefix) $\rightarrow$ **`verzorgen`** (Verb: to take care of)
    *(Note: The infinitive ending `-en` is dropped to create the stem `verzorg-` for the next step.)*

2.  **Noun Formation:**
    `verzorg-` (Verb Stem) + `-ing` (Suffix) $\rightarrow$ **`verzorging`** (Noun: care/maintenance)

### 4. Summary of Meaning
*   **Literal Construction:** "The process of providing care."
*   **Actual Usage:** Care, maintenance, upbringing, provision, or grooming (e.g., *huisdierverzorging* = pet care; *verzorging van de huid* = skin care).

### 5. Linguistic Classification
*   **Word Class:** Noun (*Zelfstandig naamwoord*)
*   **Morphological Type:** Agglutinative (morphemes are strung together with clear boundaries).
*   **Stress Pattern:** Primary stress falls on the root: *ver-**ZOR**-ging*. (Prefixes like `ver-` are unstressed in Dutch).
