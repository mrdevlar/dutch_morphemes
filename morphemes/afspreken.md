From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`afspreken`** is a **separable compound verb** (*scheidbaar werkwoord*). It is formed through the process of prefixation, where a particle is attached to a verbal root.

Here is the morphological breakdown and explanation of the morphemes:

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented into three distinct morphemes:

> **af** + **sprek** + **en**

### 2. Detailed Morpheme Analysis

#### A. `af-` (Prefix / Particle)
*   **Type:** Separable Prefix (Derivational Morpheme).
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Old Dutch *af*, cognate with German *ab* and English *off*.
*   **Core Meaning:** It generally denotes direction (away, down) or completion/conclusion.
*   **Function:** In this specific context, it acts as a **verbal particle**. It modifies the meaning of the root verb. While *spreken* means "to speak," the addition of *af-* shifts the meaning from the act of speaking to the **result** of the speaking (an agreement or conclusion).
*   **Linguistic Note:** In Dutch syntax, this is a *separable* prefix. In a main clause, it detaches and moves to the end of the sentence (e.g., *Ik **sprek** het **af***), whereas in the infinitive form provided here, it remains attached.

#### B. `sprek-` (Verbal Root / Stem)
*   **Type:** Free Morpheme (Lexical Root).
*   **Etymology:** From Old Dutch *sprekan*, Proto-Germanic *\*sprekaną*. It is a cognate of the English verb **to speak**.
*   **Core Meaning:** To talk, to utter words, to converse.
*   **Phonology:** This is the stem of the verb. Note that *spreken* is a **strong verb** (irregular). In the past tense, the vowel changes (e.g., *ik sprak*), but in the infinitive form `afspreken`, the stem remains `sprek-`.

#### C. `-en` (Suffix)
*   **Type:** Inflectional Suffix.
*   **Function:** This is the standard **infinitive marker** in Dutch. It marks the verb as the dictionary form (non-conjugated).
*   **Morphophonology:** It attaches to the stem `sprek-` to create the full infinitive stem `spreken`.

---

### 3. Semantic Composition (Meaning Construction)
Linguistically, the meaning of `afspreken` is **non-compositional** to a degree. You cannot simply translate the parts literally to get the modern meaning.

*   **Literal Composition:** "To speak off" or "To speak down."
*   **Idiomatic Meaning:** "To agree," "to arrange," or "to make an appointment."
*   **Semantic Shift:** The prefix *af-* implies a "closing" or "finalizing" of the conversation. Therefore, the word evolved to mean "speaking until an agreement is reached."

### 4. Syntactic Behavior (Morphosyntax)
A crucial linguistic feature of `afspreken` is its behavior as a **separable verb**.

*   **Infinitive:** *Ik wil met je **afspreken**.* (I want to arrange with you.)
*   **Main Clause (Finite):** *Ik **sprek** morgen met je **af**.* (I arrange with you tomorrow.)
    *   Here, the prefix `af-` separates from the stem `sprek-` and moves to the end of the clause.
*   **Past Participle:** *We hebben het **afgesproken**.* (We have agreed.)
    *   Here, the ge- prefix is inserted between the particle and the stem.

### 5. Related Derivatives
The morphological structure of `afspreken` generates other words in the Dutch lexicon:

*   **`Afspraak`** (Noun): *af-* + *sprek-* + *-a* (nominalizing suffix). Meaning: "An agreement" or "an appointment."
*   **`Spreken`** (Verb): The base verb without the prefix.
