### 1. Synchronic Analysis (Modern Dutch)
In contemporary Dutch morphology, **`aarde`** is considered a **monomorphemic** word. This means it consists of a single morpheme that cannot be divided into smaller meaningful units within the current language system.

*   **Word:** `aarde`
*   **Morpheme:** `{aarde}`
*   **Type:** Free Root (Lexical Morpheme)
*   **Part of Speech:** Noun (Common gender, singular)
*   **Meaning:** Earth, ground, soil.

**Why is it not broken down further?**
While the word ends in an *-e*, in modern Dutch this is not a productive grammatical suffix (unlike the plural *-en* in *hond* → *honden*). Therefore, the entire string `aarde` functions as one indivisible unit.
