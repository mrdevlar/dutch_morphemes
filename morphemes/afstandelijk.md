From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`afstandelijk`** is a derived adjective formed through a process of compounding and suffixation. It is composed of three distinct morphemes.

Here is the morphological breakdown and explanation:

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
The word can be segmented as follows:
> **`af`** + **`stand`** + **`-elijk`**

---

### 2. Detailed Morpheme Analysis

#### A. `af` (Prefix / Preposition)
*   **Form:** `af`
*   **Category:** Separable prefix / Preposition / Adverb.
*   **Literal Meaning:** "Off," "away," or "down."
*   **Function:** In this context, it indicates separation or direction away from a reference point.
*   **Etymology:** Derived from Proto-Germanic *\*af*, cognate with the English prefix *off-* or *a-* (as in *aboard*).
*   **Context:** It modifies the root to imply a state of being "away" or "separated."

#### B. `stand` (Root / Noun Stem)
*   **Form:** `stand`
*   **Category:** Noun stem (derived from a verb).
*   **Literal Meaning:** "Stand," "position," or "state."
*   **Function:** This is the core lexical unit. It comes from the Dutch verb **`staan`** (to stand).
*   **Etymology:** Cognate with the English noun **`stand`** and the verb **`to stand`**.
*   **Context:** When combined with `af`, it forms the noun **`afstand`** (distance). Historically, this comes from the verb *afstaan* (to stand off/give up), which nominalized into *afstand* (the state of standing off).

#### C. `-elijk` (Suffix)
*   **Form:** `-elijk` (variant of `-lijk`)
*   **Category:** Derivational Suffix.
*   **Literal Meaning:** "-ly," "-like," or "-able."
*   **Function:** This suffix converts a noun into an adjective. It indicates "having the quality of" or "characterized by."
*   **Phonology:** The insertion of the vowel **`e`** (schwa) before the `lijk` is a phonological adjustment to make the word pronounceable after a consonant cluster (like `nd`).
*   **Etymology:** Cognate with the English suffix **`-ly`** (as in *friendly*, *manly*) and the German suffix **`-lich`**.
*   **Context:** It transforms the concrete noun *afstand* (distance) into the abstract adjective *afstandelijk* (distant).

---

### 3. Semantic Composition (How the meaning builds)

The meaning of the word is constructed hierarchically:

1.  **`staan`** (to stand) $\rightarrow$ **`stand`** (a standing/position).
2.  **`af`** (away) + **`stand`** (position) = **`afstand`** (Distance).
    *   *Literal:* A standing away from something.
3.  **`afstand`** (Distance) + **`-elijk`** (having the quality of) = **`afstandelijk`**.
    *   *Literal:* Having the quality of distance.

**Semantic Shift:**
While the root refers to physical space, the adjective **`afstandelijk`** is almost exclusively used in a **metaphorical sense**. It describes a person's behavior that is emotionally reserved, aloof, or unapproachable. It implies an "emotional distance" between the subject and others.

### Summary
**`Afstandelijk`** is a **denominal adjective**. It takes the noun *afstand* (which itself is a compound of *af* + *stand*) and applies the adjectival suffix *-elijk*. Linguistically, it represents a shift from a spatial concept (physical distance) to a psychological concept (emotional aloofness).
