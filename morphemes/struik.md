*   **Free Morpheme:** It is a "free" morpheme because it can stand alone as an independent word.

*   **Old High German:** *struoh*

### 3. Explanation of the Morpheme

*   **Root:** `struik`
*   **Meaning:** A woody plant smaller than a tree, with multiple stems arising from the base.
*   **Phonology:** In standard Dutch, it is pronounced /strœy̯k/. The `ui` represents a diphthong, and the `k` is a voiceless velar plosive.

From a strict morphological standpoint, **`struik`** is an **indivisible root**. It carries the core lexical meaning of the word and does not contain any affixes (prefixes or suffixes) in its base form.
