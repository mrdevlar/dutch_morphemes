While the word is morphologically simple today, its history explains its form:
*   **Origin:** It derives from Middle Dutch **`crant`**.
*   **Original Meaning:** Originally, *crant* meant a "curl," "ring," or "wreath."

*   **Alternative Theory:** Some linguists suggest a connection to the Low German *Krant* or the French *courant* (as in *journal courant* or "current news"), but the "rolled paper" etymology is the most widely accepted regarding the phonological form.
