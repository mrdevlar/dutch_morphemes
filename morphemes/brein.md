### 2. Detailed Explanation

#### The Morpheme: `brein`
*   **Status:** This is a **lexical root**. It carries the core semantic meaning ("brain," "intellect," or "mind") and cannot be broken down further into smaller meaningful units within Modern Dutch.
*   **Category:** It is a neuter noun (*het brein*).
*   **Common Misconception:** Learners sometimes mistake the final `-in` for a suffix (similar to the feminine agent suffix in *vriendin* or *lerares*). However, in `brein`, the `-in` is **not** a morpheme; it is an integral part of the root. If you remove it, `bre` has no meaning in Dutch.

*   **Plural:** `brein` + `-en` = `breinen` (e.g., *verschillende breinen* — different brains/minds).
