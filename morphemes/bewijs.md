From a linguistic perspective, the Dutch word **`bewijs`** (meaning "proof," "evidence," or "certificate") is a **deverbal noun**. It is derived from a verb and contains both a prefix and a root, with a historical nominal suffix.

Here is the morphological breakdown and explanation of the morphemes.

### 1. Morphological Segmentation
In modern Dutch morphology, the word is typically analyzed as follows:

**`be-`** + **`wijz`** + **`-s`**

*   **`be-`**: Prefix
*   **`wijz`**: Root (Stem)
*   **`-s`**: Nominal Suffix (Historical)

---

### 2. Detailed Explanation of Morphemes

#### A. The Prefix: `be-`
*   **Function:** This is a very common Germanic prefix (cognate with English *be-* and German *be-*).
*   **Role:** It is a **transitivizing** or **derivational** prefix. When attached to a verb, it often makes an intransitive verb transitive (e.g., *lopen* "to walk" $\rightarrow$ *belopen* "to walk over/cover"). In the case of nouns, it often creates a noun indicating the result or object of the action.
*   **In `bewijs`:** It directs the action of the root. It implies "to cause to be shown" or "to point out specifically."

#### B. The Root: `wijz`
*   **Source:** This root comes from the verb **`wijzen`** (to point, to show, to indicate).
*   **Etymology:** It traces back to the Proto-Germanic **`*wīs-`**, which is related to the concept of "knowing" or "seeing."
*   **Cognates:**
    *   **English:** *Wise* (originally "to know"), *Witness* (one who shows/knows), *Weird* (via *wyrd* "fate/what is shown").
    *   **German:** *weisen* (to show), *Beweis* (proof).
*   **Meaning:** The core semantic meaning is "pointing" or "showing."

#### C. The Suffix: `-s`
*   **Function:** This is a **nominalizing suffix**.
*   **Historical Note:** In Old High German (*biwisi*) and Middle Dutch, this ending was a nominal suffix (often *-is* or *-i*) used to create abstract nouns from verbs.
*   **Modern Status:** In modern Dutch, this *-s* is no longer a productive suffix (you cannot simply add *-s* to a verb to make a noun). It is now fossilized within the word stem.
*   **Distinction:** It is crucial **not** to confuse this *-s* with the plural marker *-s* (as in *auto's*). The *-s* in *bewijs* is part of the singular stem. The plural of *bewijs* is *bewijzen*, which uses the standard plural suffix *-en*.

---

### 3. Phonological Note: Final Devoicing
A key feature of Dutch phonology is **final devoicing** (*eindverharding*).

*   **Underlying Form:** The root is **`wijz`** (voiced *z*).
*   **Surface Form:** In the word *bewijs*, the *z* appears as an **`s`** (voiceless).
*   **Rule:** In Dutch, voiced obstruents (like *b, d, v, z*) become voiceless (*p, t, f, s*) at the end of a syllable or word.
*   **Evidence:** You can see the underlying voiced *z* in the plural form **`bewijzen`** and the verb **`bewijzen`**, where the *z* is followed by a vowel and remains voiced.

### 4. Semantic Shift
The meaning of the word has evolved through a logical semantic chain:
1.  **Root (`wijzen`):** To point or show physically.
2.  **Derived Verb (`bewijzen`):** To show something to be true; to demonstrate.
3.  **Derived Noun (`bewijs`):** The thing that is shown; the demonstration itself; **proof/evidence**.

Linguistically, **`bewijs`** is a deverbal noun formed by the prefix **`be-`**, the root **`wijz`** (from *wijzen*), and a historical nominal suffix **`-s`**. The spelling reflects the Dutch rule of final devoicing, masking the voiced *z* found in related forms like *bewijzen*.
